# Career Positioning Generator

## Purpose

Turn verified candidate evidence into a concise positioning statement for a
specific opportunity.

## Prompt

Based only on the candidate evidence provided, create:

1. A one-line professional positioning statement
2. A 30-second introduction
3. Three reasons the candidate is relevant to the role
4. Three transferable strengths
5. One honest development gap
6. A recommended CV headline

The positioning must:

- be specific to the target role;
- use evidence rather than generic adjectives;
- avoid exaggeration;
- avoid inventing experience;
- distinguish direct experience from transferable experience.
