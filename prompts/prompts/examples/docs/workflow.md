# Career Acceleration Workflow

## INPUT

Candidate CV
+
Target Job Description

↓

## 1. DISCOVER

Extract:

- skills
- experience
- achievements
- qualifications
- career themes

↓

## 2. ANALYSE

Extract:

- must-have requirements
- preferred requirements
- responsibilities
- competencies

↓

## 3. MAP

Compare candidate evidence against requirements.

↓

## 4. POSITION

Generate:

- professional headline
- positioning statement
- strongest evidence
- transferable strengths

↓

## 5. PROVE

Identify:

- achievement stories
- case studies
- portfolio evidence

↓

## 6. PREPARE

Generate:

- interview stories
- likely questions
- development priorities

↓

## 7. ACCELERATE

Create:

- 30-day learning plan
- networking priorities
- targeted opportunity strategy

↓

## HUMAN REVIEW

Validate every recommendation before use.
