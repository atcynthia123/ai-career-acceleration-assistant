# Example: Identifying an Unmet Career/Education Need

## Opportunity

I noticed that many teachers wanted to understand the transition from Kenya's
CBC curriculum to the International Baccalaureate (IB) but lacked accessible,
practical resources.

## What I did

Rather than waiting for a formal request, I created a free webinar addressing
the transition.

After observing continued interest, I developed a structured CBC-to-IB course
and made it available through Selar.

## Career acceleration principle demonstrated

DISCOVER → identify an unmet need

POSITION → understand the audience and problem

PROVE → create a practical resource

CONNECT → engage educators

SCALE → turn the initial webinar into a reusable course

## AI opportunity

AI could support this workflow by helping analyse educator questions, identify
recurring themes, structure learning resources and personalise recommended
learning pathways.

Human expertise remains necessary for curriculum accuracy, context and
professional judgment.
