# Interview Story Generator

Identify experiences from the candidate's background that could answer common
behavioural interview questions.

Generate story candidates for:

1. Problem solving
2. Customer service
3. Leadership
4. Initiative
5. Conflict
6. Failure or learning
7. Innovation
8. AI adoption
9. Working with ambiguity
10. Stakeholder management

For each story use:

SITUATION
TASK
ACTION
RESULT
LEARNING

Only use facts contained in the candidate's evidence.

Do not invent metrics or outcomes.
