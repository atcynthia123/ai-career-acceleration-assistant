# AI Career Acceleration Assistant

An AI-assisted career development workflow designed to help professionals move from
generic job searching to targeted career positioning.

## The problem

Many job seekers respond to job descriptions by repeatedly rewriting their CVs and
submitting applications without systematically identifying:

- what the employer actually needs;
- which parts of their experience are most relevant;
- where evidence is missing;
- how to position their existing experience;
- how to prepare for interviews.

This project explores how AI can support that process.

## What it does

The workflow takes two inputs:

1. A candidate's CV or professional experience
2. A target job description

It then helps generate:

- Job requirement analysis
- Candidate-to-role evidence mapping
- Strengths and gaps
- Career positioning statement
- Achievement evidence
- Interview story ideas
- 30-day development priorities

## Workflow

CV + Job Description
        ↓
Requirement Extraction
        ↓
Evidence Mapping
        ↓
Strength & Gap Analysis
        ↓
Career Positioning
        ↓
Interview Preparation
        ↓
30-Day Development Plan

## AI approach

The project uses structured prompting and human review rather than treating
AI-generated recommendations as final decisions.

The user remains responsible for validating:

- experience claims;
- achievements;
- qualifications;
- job requirements;
- ethical and factual accuracy.

## Why I built it

I am interested in the intersection of AI, learning, career development and
workflow design.

The project grew from my experience helping professionals translate complex
experience into clearer career positioning and from experimenting with AI as a
thinking and productivity partner.

## Current status

Version 1 — Prompt-based prototype.

Future iterations may include:

- automated job-description parsing;
- structured scoring;
- portfolio/evidence extraction;
- interview-question generation;
- workflow automation;
- integration with a knowledge base.

## What I am learning

This project is an exploration of how AI can augment professional judgment rather
than replace it.

The focus is on building workflows where AI handles repetitive analysis while
humans provide context, judgment and final validation.
